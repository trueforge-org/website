# Kvapple

## Story / Intro

An attempt to create a balanced simple Cyser, using off-the-shelve ingredients.

## Deviations from Base Mead

This recipe lists only deviations from the Base Mead workflow. Follow the
Base Mead procedure and ingredient list, applying the deviations below.

- Juice: Use 15 L of apple juice composed of a mix of hazy (cloudy unfiltered)
  and clear pressed apple juices. Adjust the ratio to taste (example: 10 L
  hazy + 5 L clear for body and brightness).

## Tasting Notes (template)

Semi-Dry

More nuance than after-market Ciders and Cysers, a tad astringent which mutes away some of the honey and apple flavours
