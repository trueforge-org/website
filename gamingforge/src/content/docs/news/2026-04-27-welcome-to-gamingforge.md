**GamingForge now has its own dedicated website** — and, for the first time, its own news section.

## A Home of Its Own

Up until now, GamingForge was tucked into the broader TrueForge site. As part of our larger spring 2026 reorganisation, we have given GamingForge — alongside ContainerForge, ManicMeads, and TrueCharts — a dedicated home of its own.

The new [gamingforge.org](https://gamingforge.org) is focused entirely on:

- Our community **game servers** (Minecraft, ARK, and friends)
- Setup guides and player-facing documentation
- Updates, events, and announcements

## A News Section

This post is also a small milestone: **GamingForge now has a news section**.

Until now, gaming-related updates were buried in the broader TrueForge announcements (or sometimes not announced at all). From now on, anything game-server-specific — server updates, world wipes, new servers, events — will live here.

If you only care about the gaming side of TrueForge, you can now follow this site (and only this site) and not miss anything.

## Still Part of the TrueForge Family

GamingForge remains a project under the **TrueForge** umbrella. TrueForge continues to handle the unglamorous parts: hosting, infrastructure, CI/CD, and community support — so the GamingForge side can stay focused on actually running fun servers.

## What’s Next

- More server updates and event announcements landing here
- Expanded guides for joining and getting the most out of each server
- Closer tie-in with the rest of the TrueForge ecosystem (yes, our game servers run on our own tooling)

Come join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/tVsPTHWTtr) and welcome to the new GamingForge.

— The GamingForge Team
