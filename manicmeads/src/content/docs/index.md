title: About Manic Meads
---

Manic Meads is a project dedicated to documenting small batch mead making, specialising in farmhouse-style meads.

A side effort of which is focussing on cost-effectiveness and adding taste without significant expense.

## What You'll Find Here

- **[Recipes](/meads/)** — Step-by-step mead recipes, from our base workflow to experimental cysers and melomels.
- **[Articles](/articles/)** — Deep dives into yeast science, nutrient management, and fermentation experiments.
