In contrast to our other mango styles, this one has been mixed with Passionfruit and Peach, to give a much more vibrant mango flavour.

## Deviations from Base Mead

This recipe lists only deviations from the Base Mead workflow. Follow the
Base Mead procedure and ingredient list, applying the deviations below.

- Fruit: Use a combination of mango, passionfruit, and peach to create a
  vibrant tropical fruit profile that enhances the mango rather than
  overwhelming it.

## Tasting Notes

Semi-Dry

A more vibrant and tropical take on mango mead, with passionfruit and peach adding complexity and brightness.
