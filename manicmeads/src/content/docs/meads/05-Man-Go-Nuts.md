Farmhouse style semi-dry Melomel, tasting like Dried Mango, a hint of tropical sweetness and aftertaste of fresh nuts.

## Deviations from Base Mead

This recipe lists only deviations from the Base Mead workflow. Follow the
Base Mead procedure and ingredient list, applying the deviations below.

- Fruit: Use mango as the primary fruit addition.
- Yeast: Use a farmhouse-style yeast to produce a dry, funky character that
  complements the tropical fruit.

## Tasting Notes

Semi-Dry

Unexpectedly nutty results, maybe a little cardboard. The resulting taste of dried fruits nicely compliments this.
