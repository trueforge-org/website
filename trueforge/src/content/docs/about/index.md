TrueForge is a community-driven organisation that hosts and supports open-source sub-projects by joining forces. Rather than each project standing alone, TrueForge brings them together under one roof — sharing the resources, knowledge and infrastructure that every project needs but few can sustain on their own.

## What we provide

### Hosting & Infrastructure

TrueForge supplies the servers, CI/CD pipelines, container registries and other infrastructure our sub-projects depend on. Projects that join TrueForge no longer have to worry about keeping the lights on — we handle it.

### Technical Know-how

Our maintainers bring deep expertise in containerisation, Kubernetes, Helm charting, web development and more. This knowledge is pooled across all projects, so every team benefits from lessons learned elsewhere.

### Community Resources

All TrueForge projects share a single, active [Discord server](/s/discord) along with common discussion channels, support workflows and moderation. A unified community means more people helping each other, faster answers and a friendlier atmosphere for newcomers.

### Governance & Legal

TrueForge provides a shared code of conduct, licensing framework and sponsorship infrastructure, reducing the administrative burden on individual project leads.
