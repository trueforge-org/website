## Purpose

Before beginning any body composition protocol, you must establish **baseline measurements** and confirm you meet the **safety prerequisites**. Skipping this stage increases injury, health, and failure risk.

## What This Stage Covers

- Body composition assessment (weight, body fat %, circumferences)
- Baseline blood work and health screening
- Identifying contraindications
- Setting realistic targets based on current measurements
- Establishing tracking methods and frequency
